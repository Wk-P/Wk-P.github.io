02 · Thesis

Master's Thesis

Research on privacy-aware service placement in resource-constrained edge environments.

Privacy Preserving Edge Service Placement by Optimization and Safe Reinforcement Learning

Edge Computing · Optimization · Safe Reinforcement Learning
In Progress
  • Models service placement under ECU capacity and privacy-conflict constraints.
  • Uses integer optimization as a reference and compares it with sequential RL-based placement.
  • Studies PPO-based safety mechanisms together with DQN and DDQN baselines.
  • Evaluates feasibility, resource utilization, and constraint satisfaction across multiple scenarios.
