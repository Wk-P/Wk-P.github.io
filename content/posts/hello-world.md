This is the opening draft for my technical blog. I plan to use this space to document what I build, what I learn, and the questions that connect engineering with research.

## What I want to write about

- **Cloud-native systems:** Kubernetes, OpenStack, infrastructure, and observability.
- **Backend engineering:** Python services, API design, and the decisions behind a working application.
- **Research notes:** resource optimization, edge computing, and reinforcement learning.

## From projects to explanations

A repository shows the code. A blog post gives room to explain the problem, the design decisions, and the limits of a solution.

For each technical post, I want to make the context clear, include a reproducible example where possible, and distinguish observations from assumptions.
